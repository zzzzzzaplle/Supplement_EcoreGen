Retail customers should be eligible for applying IPO. Customers who will make IPO Applications must share their name, surname, email address, and telephone number information with the related company.

When a customer applies IPO, he is required to upload legal allowance documentation. Each document has a name that identifies the allowance document. When the customer completes the application process successfully, the bank should send two information emails, one for the customer and another for the company. If the customer has many failed attempts, the bank should restrict the customer from the application process. If they would like to apply anyway, they should call the "call center" and request IPO application access.

Even though the customer completed the process successfully, the purchase is still being determined. Related companies always have a right to reject customers' applications. If a company rejects the customer, our system should inform the customer. Email content should include the customer's name, surname, email address, and telephone number, the applied company name, the number of shares that have been purchased, and the amount of money that has been paid.
