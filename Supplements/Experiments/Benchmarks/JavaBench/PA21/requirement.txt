1. Overview
This project implements a grid-based puzzle game called Inertia. The core mechanic is inertial sliding: once the player chooses a direction, the character slides continuously in that direction until it hits a Wall or enters a StopCell. The objective is to navigate the board to collect all Gems while avoiding Mines that cause death and life loss. The game features a scoring system, life management, and an Undo system to backtrack moves.
2. Functional Requirements
The system manages 2D grid coordinates and their deltas through the Position and PositionOffset classes for all movement calculations and boundary checks.
In GameBoardController, when a move is initiated via a Direction, the player piece moves step-by-step from its current Position. If the player successfully slides at least one cell and the movement ends by a normal stopping condition, such as a Wall, boundary, or StopCell, without hitting a Mine, the result is Alive. During an Alive move, any Gem or ExtraLife passed through is collected, removed from the board, and recorded in the Alive result.
Sliding stops immediately before a Wall, at the board boundary, or exactly on a StopCell. StopCell is a special cell type that can only contain a Player entity. Hitting a Mine causes a Dead result and stops movement. The position recorded for the player after a Dead move is the original position. The board remains unchanged from before the move, and any Gem or ExtraLife passed before the Mine is not collected or removed.
A move returns Invalid when the player cannot move at all, such as when blocked by a Wall or boundary on the first step, resulting in zero movement distance. Every move must return a MoveResult: Alive, Dead, or Invalid. Collected Gems and ExtraLives are recorded only in Alive results.
In GameState, the remaining gem count is the number of Gems still present on the GameBoard. A player wins when there are no Gems left on the board. A player loses when lives reach zero, unless in unlimited lives mode.
For scoring, the number of collected Gems is initialNumOfGems minus the remaining gem count, and the score is calculated as:
Score = InitialBoardSize + (CollectedGems * 10) - (Moves * 1) - (Undoes * 2) - (Deaths * 4).
If numLives is less than zero, the player has unlimited lives, and the reported life count is Integer.MAX_VALUE.
In GameController, Invalid moves do not change counters and are not pushed to the MoveStack. Alive moves increment numMoves, apply collected ExtraLife effects, and are pushed to the MoveStack. Dead moves increment numMoves and numDeaths, decrease finite lives by one, and are not pushed to the MoveStack. Only Alive moves are undoable.
Undo pops one Alive move if available, restores the player position and collected Gems/ExtraLives, reverses finite ExtraLife gains, and returns true. If no move is available, it returns false. Undo does not decrement numMoves; undo count is represented by MoveStack.popCount.
GameStateSerializer is a tool for loading and saving the game state from/to text format, handling the mapping between characters W, P, S, G, M, and L and Ecore classes.
InertiaTextGame is the main runner handling the STDIN loop and parsing user commands like U, D, Undo, or Quit.
